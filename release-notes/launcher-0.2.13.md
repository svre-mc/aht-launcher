## What changed

- Minecraft starting the exact configured A Hard Time instance now completes Play even when Minecraft Launcher closes or hides its window during handoff.
- The active, signed Play proof remains available after AHT Launcher closes so the client can present it when connecting to the server.
- Launcher reports no longer claim Minecraft Launcher failed when the configured modpack is already running.
- Phoenix Anti-cheat authorization remains bound to the same one-time Play session throughout the corrected handoff.
