## Account sync and update fixes

- Account recovery now skips expired or damaged cached sessions and tries other valid sessions for the matching Minecraft account.
- Launcher updates wait for you to choose Update now before downloading.
- Fixed the update prompt flashing over the loading screen or disappearing during progress updates.
- Restart remains available only after the update is verified and fully prepared.
- Matching modpack/server update verifies admission before allowing world access.
- Improved Windows verification efficiency without removing client-integrity checks.

Windows, universal macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon), and Linux x64 builds.
