# AHT launcher font provenance

`SourceSerif4-Regular.ttf` and `SourceSerif4-Semibold.ttf` are the exact source
files used to generate the AHT in-game Aurora `BODY` and `BODY_BOLD` bitmap
font atlases. They were copied from the private AHT commerce UI design owner:

`aht-server-utilities-commerce-revamp/Project-async-commerce-1.0.10-current-r1/design/aurora-circuit-v2/fonts`

Source Serif 4 is published by Adobe under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

`Bender-Regular.otf`, `Bender-Light.otf`, and `Bender-Bold.otf` are the
unmodified Bender family by Jovanny Lemonad. The family was downloaded from
the Bender distribution at `https://www.1001fonts.com/bender-font.html` and is
published under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The complete license supplied
with that distribution is retained as `Bender-OFL-1.1.txt`.
